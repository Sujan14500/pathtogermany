# 08 · Exam formats: the Goethe parts the app's content and tests follow

The app's texts, recordings and tasks are original; only the *format* follows the Goethe-Zertifikat. Each piece
of content names its part (`part: "Teil 2"`), so practice tests and mock exams can assemble a paper in the real
order. Timings and part counts follow the published exam descriptions; check them against the official
Übungssätze (linked in the app, never copied).

| Level | Lesen | Hören | Schreiben | Sprechen | Pass |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 (Start Deutsch 1) | 3 parts, 25 min | 3 parts, about 20 min | 2 parts, 20 min | 3 parts, about 15 min (group) | 60% overall |
| A2 | 4 parts, 30 min | 4 parts, about 30 min | 2 parts, 30 min | 3 parts, about 15 min (pairs) | 60% overall |
| B1 | 5 parts, 65 min | 4 parts, about 40 min | 3 parts, 60 min | 3 parts, about 15 min (pairs) | 60% in each module |

## Lesen (reading)

Question formats: `rf` (richtig/falsch), `jn` (ja/nein), `abc` (three options), `match` (choose the ad/text
by its label; `x` = none fits, B1 Teil 3 only).

- **A1 Teil 1** · two short personal texts (note, email, text message), 40–90 words each · 5 × `rf`.
- **A1 Teil 2** · small ads or web snippets, two per situation · 5 × `match` with two options (a/b): "Where do you
  find the information?".
- **A1 Teil 3** · signs, notices, short announcements on a door or board · 5 × `rf`.
- **A2 Teil 1** · short newspaper or magazine article, 120–200 words · 5 × `abc`.
- **A2 Teil 2** · an information board or directory (department store, town hall, school) · 5 × `abc` ("Where do
  you go?").
- **A2 Teil 3** · an email or letter, 120–180 words · 5 × `abc`.
- **A2 Teil 4** · 6 short ads (a–f), 5 people looking for something · 5 × `match`.
- **B1 Teil 1** · blog or diary entry, 200–300 words · 6 × `rf`.
- **B1 Teil 2** · a newspaper article, 200–300 words · 3 × `abc` (a paper has two such texts).
- **B1 Teil 3** · 8–10 short ads (a–j), 7 situations · 7 × `match`, one situation has no fitting ad (`x`).
- **B1 Teil 4** · 7 short reader comments on one question (40–70 words each) · 7 × `jn` ("Is the writer for
  …?").
- **B1 Teil 5** · house rules, terms or instructions, 150–250 words · 4 × `abc`.

## Hören (listening)

Each recording is played twice in the exam, except where marked once. Voices: `A` (a woman), `B` (a man), `N`
(an announcer or narrator).

- **A1 Teil 1** · 6 short everyday dialogues · 1 × `abc` each.
- **A1 Teil 2** · 4 announcements (station, shop, airport), played once · 1 × `rf` each.
- **A1 Teil 3** · 5 phone messages (answering machine) · 1 × `abc` each.
- **A2 Teil 1** · 5 short radio items (news, weather, traffic, ads), played once · 1 × `abc` each.
- **A2 Teil 2** · one longer conversation; 5 people or days to match to a choice · 5 × `match`.
- **A2 Teil 3** · 5 short everyday dialogues · 1 × `abc` each.
- **A2 Teil 4** · a radio interview · 5 × `jn` ("Did the guest say …?").
- **B1 Teil 1** · 5 short texts (messages, announcements, radio) · 2 questions each: `rf` then `abc`.
- **B1 Teil 2** · a guided tour or talk, played once · 5 × `abc`.
- **B1 Teil 3** · a conversation between two people, played once · 7 × `rf`.
- **B1 Teil 4** · a radio discussion with a host and two guests · 8 × `match` (who says it: host, guest 1, guest 2).

## Schreiben (writing)

- **A1 Teil 1** · fill in a form (5 fields) from a short text about another person.
- **A1 Teil 2** · a short message (about 30 words) covering 3 given points.
- **A2 Teil 1** · a text message (20–30 words) covering 3 points.
- **A2 Teil 2** · an email (30–40 words) covering 3 points, informal or semi-formal.
- **B1 Teil 1** · a personal email (about 80 words) covering 3 points.
- **B1 Teil 2** · a forum post giving your opinion (about 80 words).
- **B1 Teil 3** · a short semi-formal email (about 40 words), e.g. apologising or asking for something.

## Sprechen (speaking)

- **A1 Teil 1** · introduce yourself (Name, Alter, Land, Wohnort, Sprachen, Beruf, Hobby), then spell a word
  and say a number.
- **A1 Teil 2** · a topic and word cards: ask a question with the word, answer your partner's question.
- **A1 Teil 3** · picture cards: make a request and react to one.
- **A2 Teil 1** · question cards: ask your partner about their life and answer theirs.
- **A2 Teil 2** · talk about yourself on a topic card with four prompts.
- **A2 Teil 3** · plan something together (two diaries, find a time).
- **B1 Teil 1** · plan something together (what, when, where, who brings what).
- **B1 Teil 2** · a presentation on a topic, guided by 5 slides (your experience, the situation in your country,
  pros and cons, your opinion, a closing).
- **B1 Teil 3** · react to the partner's presentation: give feedback and ask a question, answer theirs.

Writing and speaking in the app's mock exams are self-scored against the rubric; the app says so.
