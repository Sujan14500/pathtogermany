# Characters: the story bible

The whole course (A1 → A2 → B1) follows one storyline in **Köln**. Every text, example sentence and question that
names a recurring character must match the tables below; all of `data/src` was brought in line with them.

## Rules for writers

- **Recurring characters keep their facts.** Don't change an age, a home, a job, a partner or a family member to
  fit a sentence. If the sentence needs a different fact, give it to a one-off person (*mein Bruder*, *eine Kollegin
  von Malte*, or a new name).
- **One-off names are new names.** Before you use one, search `data/src` for it. Never reuse a cast name or a cast
  surname (see the last table), and never give a surname that is already used to a different person. A shop or firm
  that appears twice must be the same shop (Bäckerei Krause, Fahrrad Schulz, Kaufhaus Becker, Schuhgeschäft Lorenz).
- **Time is relative.** Year 1 is the year Priya arrives. Station order follows the year only roughly; when a text
  says "seit … / vor …", use the timeline at the end.
- **"You"** (Sie/du in writing and speaking tasks) are a member of Herr Weber's course who works in Malte's team and
  lives in Bergstraße 9 too, so Frau Yilmaz is your neighbour and Herr Krüger your landlord.
